# Arth

A live Indian stock market dashboard being built in stages — from a basic data display app to a full AI-powered research assistant.

"Arth" (अर्थ) means wealth, meaning, and purpose in Sanskrit.

---

## What It Is

Arth is a build-in-public project. The idea is to start simple, ship each stage, and keep adding meaningful functionality over time — rather than building the final product all at once. Each stage stands on its own and adds something new on top of what came before.

---

## Stages

| Stage | Focus |
|-------|-------|
| **1** | Live market dashboard — stock search, quotes, charts, indices, market movers |
| **2** | Portfolio tracker — holdings, cost basis, P&L |
| **3** | News & sentiment — ticker-linked news, bullish/bearish scoring |
| **4** | AI research chat — natural language queries about the market |
| **5** | Financial models & screener — DCF, Monte Carlo, stock screener |
| **6** | Multi-agent research system — specialized agents working in parallel |

---

## Current Stage

**Stage 1 is complete.**

The app currently lets you search any NSE/BSE stock, view live prices and key stats, explore historical price charts (line and candlestick), see top market movers, track a watchlist, and monitor live indices — all powered by a FastAPI backend with zero paid APIs.

---

## Stack (Stage 1)

- **Backend:** Python, FastAPI, yfinance
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Chart.js
